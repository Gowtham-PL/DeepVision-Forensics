## Executive Summary & Scientific Rigor Standard

To ensure forensic validity and prevent overclaiming in research publications, every metadata field, device label, generative model label, and transmission claim in the **E19 Smartphone Benchmark** was audited directly against the upstream primary sources, raw archive directories, and construction scripts.

The audit establishes four distinct epistemic levels across all claims:
1. **[SUPPORTED FACT]**: Explicitly documented and confirmed by primary upstream dataset cards, repository archives, and cryptographic metadata.
2. **[SOURCE-DERIVED METADATA]**: Confirmed at the family/category level by upstream repository tags and user uploads, but lacking sub-variant or engine-level granularity per file.
3. **[SIMULATED TRANSFORMATION]**: Mathematically controlled local reproduction of real-world degradation parameters; not transmitted through proprietary live networks.
4. **[UNKNOWN / NOT VERIFIED]**: Missing or ambiguous upstream granularity that cannot be scientifically inferred.

## 3. WhatsApp Transformation Audit

### Critical Finding: Controlled Simulation, Not Live Network Transit

- **Implementation in Code**:
  `apply_whatsapp_pipeline(img)` in `build_e19_benchmark.py`:
  - Resized long edge to 1280 px with bilinear interpolation.
  - Saved as JPEG with Quality Factor $Q=75$.
  - Enforced 4:2:0 chroma subsampling (`subsampling="4:2:0"`).
  - Stripped all EXIF metadata and ICC color profiles (`optimize=True`, clean byte buffer).

- **Epistemic Classification**: **[SIMULATED TRANSFORMATION]**
  These images did **not** traverse the live WhatsApp cellular network or servers. Rather, they were transformed using an offline, deterministic simulation parameterization matching WhatsApp's known production image pipeline.

- **Correction Implemented**:
  Every occurrence of the phrase `"authentic WhatsApp transmission pipeline"` in documentation, manifests, and integrity reports has been replaced with:
  > **`controlled WhatsApp-style compression simulation (1280px long edge, JPEG Q75, 4:2:0 chroma subsampling, stripped EXIF/ICC)`**

- **Total WhatsApp-Style Processed**: Exactly **60 images (30.0%)**
  - **Real**: 30 images (11 iPhone X, 10 Samsung S9, 9 Google Pixel)
