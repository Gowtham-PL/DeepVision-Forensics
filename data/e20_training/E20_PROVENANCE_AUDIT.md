## 1. Provenance Verification Protocol

Each data source integrated into the E20 training and development splits was audited against three mandatory criteria:
1. **Source Authenticity**: Direct download from an established, reputable research repository or public domain archive with verifiable upstream lineage.
2. **Metadata Fidelity**: Strict adherence to documented device models, camera hardware, and generative model versions. No speculative versioning or device assignment was permitted.
3. **Legal Research Compliance**: Verification that open licenses (Apache 2.0, CC BY, CC0, Public Domain, or explicit Academic Research Use grants) govern the distribution.

## 4. Hardware & Generator Non-Speculation Compliance

In adherence to strict scientific constraints:
- **No Speculative Attribution**: No image was assigned a generator name or camera model based on visual heuristics.
- **Version Traceability**: Every record in `e20_train_manifest.csv` and `e20_dev_manifest.csv` carries the exact source dataset, provenance URL/path, and verified metadata string.
- **Null Values**: Device fields for AI images are explicitly set to `"none"`, and generator fields for Real camera images are explicitly set to `"none"`.
